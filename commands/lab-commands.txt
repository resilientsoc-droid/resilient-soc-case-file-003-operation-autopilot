-------------------------------------------------------------

  "http://192.168.1.11:9001/cortex/api/analyzer/c4538ef31090f3530d1f6e2b333ad580/run" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <CORTEX_API_KEY>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
        "data": "8.8.8.8",
        "dataType": "ip",
        "tlp": 2,
        "force": 1
      }'

Common failure modes when adapting this call (see Section 17 of the lab guide):
  - Omitting "/cortex" from the URL path        -> 404
  - Omitting the Authorization header             -> 401
  - Omitting Content-Type: application/json       -> 400
